How requests work

01

Join the private channel

Pay for the club, link Discord to your website account, and receive the Target Raffle Club Discord role.

02

Choose any items

The owner posts each item's price. Request one, several, or no products, and choose your quantity separately for each.

03

Watch your queue position

Requests are ordered by the time they are confirmed. Everyone in the club can view the line. Editing your quantity keeps your place; leaving and rejoining moves you to the end.

Joining a queue is a request, not a completed purchase, reserved inventory, or guarantee of allocation. The owner confirms stock availability, fulfillment, and item payment separately. Club fees renew monthly until cancelled.
