How requests work

01

Join the private channel

Pay for the club, link Discord to your website account, and receive the Target Raffle Club Discord role.

02

Select SKUs, then pay

The owner posts the price of each SKU. Choose one or several products in Discord, then pay the combined item total on this website. The monthly club fee is separate.

03

Enter the paid queue

Your queue position is recorded after Stripe confirms your payment. Other club members can see the order of paid requests. Further paid purchases enter later in the queue.

If a Target draw order is canceled, payments for the canceled raffle items will be refunded. If only some items or quantities are canceled, a partial refund may be issued. A paid raffle selection does not guarantee retailer fulfillment. Your club subscription renews monthly until canceled and is a separate charge from merchandise.
