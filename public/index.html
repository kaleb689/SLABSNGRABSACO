ACO SETUP GUIDE

Prepare your profile, payment method, and supported email connection.

Create your ACO profile with your shipping and contact information. Use a clear profile name so it is easy to identify.

Step 2 — Add a Payment Card

Use the payment method intended for your ACO profile and verify the billing information matches the card issuer.

For retailers that require email verification, use a dedicated email account and provider-supported app password when available.
