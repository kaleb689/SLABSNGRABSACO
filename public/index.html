R Renting Accounts — How Rental Accounts Work ›

Need more accounts for a drop?

Rental accounts are optional, pre-created retailer accounts that are SMS verified and ready for ACO. You choose the retailer, the number of accounts, and how long you want to rent them.

Rentals are retailer-specific

Target and Walmart rentals are separate. A Target rental account cannot be used as a Walmart account, and a Walmart rental account cannot be used as a Target account.

1

Choose your retailer

Open My Profile → Rental Availability and choose Target or Walmart.

2

Choose 5, 10, or 15 accounts

The live inventory counter shows how many accounts are currently available for each retailer.

3

Choose the rental length

Select 1 Drop, 1 Week, or 1 Month. The rental price updates automatically based on your choices.

4

Complete the account information

After the rental is assigned, add or select the shipping address and card information you want used for each rented account. Retailer login credentials remain private and are not shown on your customer page.

Multiple-account recommendation

For larger rental packages, using more than one payment method and more than one shipping address can help spread activity across accounts. The best setup can vary by drop and retailer.

01 Chapter 01 — Retailer Account Setup ›

Retailer Login Information

For every retailer you want SLABSNGRABSACO to use, you need to provide the login information for that retailer account.

Retailer login credentials are NOT your IMAP credentials

Your retailer email and password are used to sign in to the store itself. Your IMAP email and app password are used for your email inbox. They serve completely different purposes.

RETAILER LOGIN

Store Account

Used to log into Target, Walmart, Sam's Club, Costco, or another supported retailer.

  • Retailer login email / username
  • Retailer account password
IMAP LOGIN

Email Inbox

Used when email access is needed for order confirmations or verification messages.

  • Your email address
  • Your email app password
1

Make sure your retailer accounts work

Before submitting a retailer profile, open that retailer's website and make sure you can successfully sign in using the email and password you plan to submit.

2

Submit credentials for each retailer you want to use

Each retailer profile has its own login fields. Enter the correct login email or username and password for that specific retailer.

  • Target: Target account email / username + password
  • Walmart: Walmart account email / username + password
  • Sam's Club: Sam's Club account email / username + password
  • Costco: Costco account email / username + password
  • PKC: Complete the PKC fields required by the profile form.
Multiple profiles

If your membership allows multiple ACO profiles, complete the correct retailer credentials for each profile separately.

02 Chapter 02 — Email, IMAP & App Passwords ›

IMAP Setup

Some retailer setups may require access to an email inbox so order confirmations and verification messages can be detected.

What IMAP information means

Your IMAP email is the email inbox being connected. Your IMAP app password is a special password generated by your email provider. It is not your normal email account password.

1

Sign in to your email account

Open your email provider in a browser and sign into the email account you plan to use for ACO.

Make sure you can access the inbox before continuing.

2

Enable 2-Step Verification

For Gmail, open your Google Account and go to:

Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification

Follow Google's instructions and make sure 2-Step Verification is fully enabled.

If App Passwords is unavailable

First confirm that 2-Step Verification has been completely enabled on your Google account.

3

Open Google App Passwords

After 2-Step Verification is enabled, open the App Passwords section of your Google account.

Google Account → Security → App Passwords

4

Create a new App Password

Enter a name that will help you recognize the connection later.

Examples include:

  • SLABSNGRABSACO
  • Target
  • Walmart
  • ACO Email

After entering a name, select Create.

5

Copy the generated App Password

Google will display a generated app password. Copy the entire code before closing the window.

Do not use your normal Gmail password

The IMAP App Password field should contain the generated app password, not the password you normally use to sign into Gmail.

6

Enter the IMAP information on SLABSNGRABSACO

Return to your profile setup form.

Enter:

  • IMAP / Host Email: the email address connected to the inbox
  • IMAP / Host App Password: the app password generated in the previous step
Quick reminder

RETAILER EMAIL + RETAILER PASSWORD = store login.

IMAP EMAIL + APP PASSWORD = email inbox connection.

01 How does SLABSNGRABSACO work? ›

You provide the information needed to create your ACO profiles, including your retailer accounts, shipping information, payment information, and any required email connection information.

Your profiles are then used with our automated checkout setup for supported retailer drops.

02 What does ACO mean? ›

ACO means Auto Checkout. It uses automated checkout tools to attempt purchases during supported product releases.

03 Are checkouts guaranteed? ›

No. ACO does not guarantee a successful checkout on every release.

Success can depend on product stock, retailer traffic, retailer restrictions, account status, profile quantity, and other conditions during the drop.

04 What retailers are supported? ›

Current memberships support profiles for Target, Walmart, Sam's Club, Costco, and PKC.

The number of profiles you can create depends on your membership tier.

05 What information do I need for a retailer profile? ›

You will need the information requested in the profile form, which may include shipping, billing, payment, contact information, and retailer login credentials.

For retailers requiring an account login, provide the login email or username and password for that specific retailer.

06 Is my retailer password the same as my IMAP password? ›

No. These are completely separate.

Your retailer email and password are used to sign into the retailer account such as Target or Walmart.

Your IMAP email and app password are used for the email inbox connection when email access is required.

07 What is an IMAP app password? ›

An app password is a separate password generated by your email provider for use with an application or service.

If your setup requires IMAP, enter the generated app password instead of your normal email account password.
