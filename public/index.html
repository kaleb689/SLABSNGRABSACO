GETTING STARTED

ACO SETUP GUIDE.

Follow these steps to prepare your profiles, payment methods, and supported email connections.

✦
Important

ACO runs pulls for every account marked “Active.” Make sure only the accounts you want checked are active.

♧ Step 1 — Set Up a Profile ›

Profiles store your personal and shipping information. Each ACO account is linked to a profile so you don't have to type the same address repeatedly.

  1. Go to Configuration → Profiles in the sidebar.
  2. Click Add Profile and complete the form: Profile Name (a label you'll recognize, such as “Main Profile”), First Name, Last Name, Phone Number, and full shipping address.
  3. Click Create Profile . You can create as many profiles as needed — typically one per shipping address.
Before you continue:

You must have at least one profile before creating an ACO account. Profiles can be reused across multiple accounts.

▣ Step 2 — Add a Payment Card ›

Payment cards may be stored centrally in the ACO application's Payment Methods area and linked to one or more ACO accounts.

  1. Go to Configuration → Payment Methods and select Add Card .
  2. Enter a Card Company, Card Number/EXP, Name on Card, and the requested card details in the official ACO application.
  3. Save the card and link it only to the accounts you intend to use.
Security reminder:

Never send full card numbers, CVVs, or expiration dates to SLABSNGRABSACO by email. Use the official ACO application and its security controls.

✉ Step 3 — Set Up an IMAP Profile (Walmart, Target & Sam's Club) ›

Walmart, Target, and Sam's Club may send order confirmations and two-factor authentication messages by email. The checkout software may need inbox access to read those messages.

  1. Go to Configuration → IMAP Profiles and click Add IMAP Profile .
  2. Give the profile a Label, enter the Email Address, choose your Email Host from the dropdown, and enter the provider-specific App Password.
  3. Save the profile, then click Test IMAP to verify the connection.
Use an app password:

Use a dedicated app password rather than your regular email password. Follow your email provider's official security instructions to create one.

Note:

Pokémon Center accounts may not need an IMAP profile. Sam's Club accounts may require IMAP depending on the setup.

✉ Why IMAP? — Understanding Email Access ›

IMAP is an email-access protocol that lets approved software read messages in an inbox without needing to operate your entire email account.

  • It can help the software locate order confirmations and verification messages.
  • Use a dedicated email address when practical.
  • Enable two-factor authentication with your email provider.
  • Use an app password with limited access when your provider supports it.
  • Remove access and rotate credentials if you stop using the integration.
Privacy tip:

Only grant access to an inbox you control, and review what messages the software can access before connecting it.

⌕ How To — Generate an App Password REQUIRED FOR IMAP ›

App-password steps vary by provider. Use the official help page for your email service and never share the generated password with anyone who does not need it.

  1. Open your email provider's account-security settings.
  2. Enable two-step verification if required.
  3. Find the App Passwords or equivalent option.
  4. Create a dedicated app password for the IMAP connection.
  5. Copy it into the official ACO application's IMAP profile field — not into this website.
Keep credentials private:

SLABSNGRABSACO does not need your regular email password, app password, card number, or CVV to process a membership payment.
